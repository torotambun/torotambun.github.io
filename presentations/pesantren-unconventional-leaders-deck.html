How ‘Pesantren’ Foster
Unconventional Leaders

Pesantren, often stereotyped as conservative religious schools, are actually incubating a psychologically distinct leadership profile — one that maps onto the Big Five traits and Francesca Gino’s rebel talents — a model Indonesian leadership discourse consistently overlooks.

Dr. Toronata Tambun
A graduate of Harvard Business School and an affiliated alumnus of MIT Sloan School of Management.
© 2026 Dr. Toronata Tambun
01  ·  Introduction

A climate-simulation workshop inside a Yogyakarta pesantren.

An MIT En-Roads Climate Simulation workshop, hosted inside a pesantren, becomes the entry point into a set of firsthand interviews with santri and caretakers.

Situation

Pesantren are widely perceived, including within Indonesia, as conservative institutions centered on rote religious learning, separate from contemporary global challenges.

Complication

A groundbreaking MIT En-Roads workshop and firsthand interviews reveal santri engaged in vibrant, norm-challenging discussion, becoming climate-justice advocates and tech innovators — directly contradicting the stereotype.

Question

What leadership model is actually being cultivated inside pesantren, and why has it gone largely unstudied?

Answer

Pesantren combine Islamic akhlak with the nine-trait Profil Santri Indonesia in ways that align closely with the Big Five personality traits and Francesca Gino’s rebel-talent framework — producing leaders who are resilient, curious, ethically grounded, and environmentally conscious.

02  ·  Gap

Leadership discourse rarely looks inside the pesantren gate.

The conflation of “religious” with “conservative” keeps a genuinely unconventional leadership pedagogy out of mainstream education and business discourse.

Core Gap

Pesantren, often misconceived as conservative, are actually at the forefront of marrying Islamic teachings with modern educational demands — nurturing leaders who are academically sound and equipped with resilience and innovative thinking. Yet leadership-development conversations in business schools and national education policy rarely name pesantren as a source of pedagogy worth studying.

© 2026 Dr. Toronata Tambun
03  ·  Framework

Three Forces Behind the Pesantren Leadership Model

Each dimension is distinct; together they explain why pesantren graduates carry an unconventional leadership signature.

01
Tradition–Modernity Fusion

Islamic environmental stewardship and akhlak (moral character) are taught alongside climate science and technology — treating innovation as a tool for tradition’s evolution, not a threat to it.

02
Psychological Alignment

The nine-trait Profil Santri Indonesia maps onto the Big Five (Openness, Conscientiousness, Agreeableness) and Gino’s rebel talents (Novelty, Curiosity, Authenticity) — a deliberate, if unnamed, personality-development curriculum.

03
Diversity as Pedagogy

Students from varied regional, socioeconomic, and cultural backgrounds collaborate directly, building empathy and global citizenship as a structural feature of pesantren life, not an add-on.

04  ·  Details

Three Cases That Ground the Argument

A climate workshop, a moral-character curriculum, and a green-pesantren practice each illustrate the fusion in action.

The En-Roads Climate Simulation workshop

MIT’s En-Roads workshop, hosted in a Yogyakarta pesantren, ignites curiosity among santri, transforming them into climate-justice advocates and tech innovators — a concrete pedagogical bridge between Islamic stewardship and global science.

The fearless, norm-challenging classroom

Interviews reveal dynamic discussions marked by a willingness to challenge norms and exchange opinions — a culture distinct from typical Indonesian student environments, and directly tied to curiosity and openness traits.

Green-pesantren practice

Santri apply environmental consciousness from personal habits like water conservation to community-level waste management, treating nature as a sacred trust under the concept of Khalifah on Earth — not merely a resource for exploitation.

05  ·  Evidence

Four Sources Behind the Argument

Each citation grounds a specific claim in the piece about what pesantren education actually builds.

EVIDENCE A
Profil Santri Indonesia

Majelis Masyayikh (2022) outlines nine Santri traits — from Pembelajar Sepanjang Hayat (lifelong learner) to Pemberani dan Berjiwa Pelopor (brave and pioneering) — as the formal basis of the pesantren character curriculum.

EVIDENCE B
The Big Five personality traits

Openness, Conscientiousness, Extraversion, Agreeableness, and Emotional Stability provide the psychological framework the piece uses to interpret santri traits in modern terms.

EVIDENCE C
Francesca Gino’s rebel talent

Gino’s framework — Novelty, Curiosity, Perspective, Diversity, Authenticity — is used to interpret pesantren’s embrace of inquiry, vulnerability, and norm-challenging as deliberate leadership formation.

EVIDENCE D
The MIT En-Roads workshop

The workshop is cited directly as the concrete, observed case that anchors the piece’s claim about pesantren’s capacity for climate and technology leadership.

06  ·  Implications

Diagnosis: Perception vs. What Pesantren Actually Build

The outside stereotype and the observed reality point in opposite directions.

✗ The Outside Perception
✗Rote memorization, resistant to change, disconnected from global challenges
✗Religious education seen as incompatible with progressive, scientific thought
✓ What Pesantren Actually Cultivate
✓Climate-literate, technology-embracing santri equipped for innovation
✓A deliberate blend of moral character, curiosity, and resilience mapped onto recognized psychological frameworks
✓Globally aware, empathetic leaders formed through structural diversity, not incidental exposure

07  ·  Conclusion

Naming and Scaling a Leadership Model

Three recommendations for taking pesantren’s leadership pedagogy seriously.

1

Document pesantren leadership pedagogy formally, so other educational institutions can study and adapt its methods.

2

Scale partnerships like the MIT En-Roads workshop across more pesantren, pairing Islamic environmental stewardship with global climate science.

3

Introduce simulation-based case studies to further cultivate santri sense-making abilities for future complexity.

Closing

In fostering the kind of holistic, transformative education pesantren exemplify, we don’t just prepare individuals for the world; we prepare the world for a brighter, more inclusive, and innovative future.
