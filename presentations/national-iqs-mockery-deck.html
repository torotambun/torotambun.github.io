Understanding National IQs and
Rejecting the Indonesian Mockery

A national IQ score of 87 has been weaponized online into comparisons with chimpanzees — a baseless, dehumanizing practice that misreads what the number actually measures: not innate intelligence, but the structural strength of a country's education, health, and economic systems.

Dr. Toronata Tambun
HBS Alumni and Affiliated Alumni of MIT Sloan School of Management.

01  ·  Introduction

A number, misread into an insult.

The mockery says more about the mocker's understanding of statistics than about the country being mocked.

Situation

An alarming trend in public discourse and media has emerged where national IQ scores from countries like Indonesia — drawn from Lynn and Mesenberg's 2024 study of 108 nations — are compared to the intelligence of animals such as chimpanzees.

Complication

This practice is baseless and dehumanizing, reflecting a fundamental misunderstanding of what national IQ scores actually represent: not innate individual intelligence, but structural factors like education quality, public health, and socio-economic conditions.

Question

What does Indonesia's national IQ of 87 actually mean, and how should it be discussed responsibly?

Answer

It exemplifies a country with significant room for improvement in educational infrastructure and access — a call for constructive discussion on education, health, and equitable opportunity, not harmful comparison.

02  ·  Gap

The number is treated as fixed. It reflects a changeable system.

Confusing the score for the ceiling is the whole error.

Core Gap

National IQ scores — 87 for Indonesia, 94 for Vietnam, comparable ranges for India, Iran, the Philippines, Brazil, Kuwait, Mexico, and Mauritius — are averages derived from cognitive tests, on a scale averaging 100 with a standard deviation of 15. Viewing these scores as definitive or innate is misleading; they should be read as indicators of the structural factors — education funding, teacher welfare, employment, health protections, anti-corruption measures — that support or hinder cognitive development. That distinction is precisely what the online mockery erases.

03  ·  Framework

What High-IQ Nations Actually Share

Not genetics — systems, applied through Kluckhohn and Strodtbeck's cultural-value lens.

01
Human Nature Orientation

A belief in continuous growth and learning supports investment in critical thinking and lifelong education; where this orientation is weak, individuals are valued only for economic or voting roles.

02
Time Orientation

Finland and Germany treat education as a future-focused, long-term investment; a present-focused orientation elsewhere undervalues strategic planning and the resilience deep learning requires.

03
Relational & Activity Orientation

Western Europe and Scandinavia balance individual achievement with community welfare and active, hands-on learning — without sacrificing merit or undervaluing vocational training.

04  ·  Details

The PISA Numbers Behind the National IQ Score

2022 PISA results, the concrete data behind the abstract number.

Indonesia: 359 reading, 366 math, 383 science

Significantly below the OECD averages of 476, 472, and 485 respectively — a gap reflecting critical thinking and practical knowledge application, not innate capacity.

Singapore: 543 reading, 575 math, 561 science

Singapore's national IQ of 108 to 110 reflects an education system prioritizing teacher quality, rigorous standards, and resource allocation — the structural factors the piece argues actually drive the score.

Malaysia and the Philippines, in between

Malaysia scores moderately better than Indonesia (388 reading, 409 math, 416 science); the Philippines shares similar challenges (355, 347, 365) — a regional pattern, not an isolated case.

05  ·  Evidence

Sources Behind the Argument

Peer-reviewed national IQ research paired with international assessment data.

EVIDENCE A
Lynn & Mesenberg (2024), "National IQs Calculated and Validated for 108 Nations"

This study, building on earlier Lynn and Vanhanen work, supplies the national IQ estimates the piece both cites and contextualizes as structural indicators rather than measures of innate intelligence.

EVIDENCE B
2022 PISA results

Reading, mathematics, and science scores for Indonesia, Singapore, Malaysia, and the Philippines ground the piece's comparative account of educational infrastructure across the region.

EVIDENCE C
Inglehart & Welzel; Ogburn's cultural lag

The claim that meaningful cultural and educational shifts take 50-75 years, or two to three generations, frames the piece's argument that quick fixes cannot substitute for sustained investment.

06  ·  Implications

Diagnosis: Mockery or Measurement?

The same number supports two entirely different conversations.

✗ Treating IQ as Fixed and Innate
✗Using a national average as a basis for dehumanizing comparisons about an entire population's intelligence
✗Ignoring the structural factors — education funding, health, employment — that the score actually reflects
✓ Treating IQ as a Structural Indicator
✓Reading the score alongside PISA results as a diagnostic for where educational investment is most needed
✓Pairing teacher welfare and curriculum reform with the broader economic and health systems that support cognitive development
✓Committing to a multi-generational timeline for cultural and educational change, rather than expecting quick results

07  ·  Conclusion

From Mockery to a Constructive Education Agenda

Four priorities that treat the score as a signal, not a verdict.

1

Pair immediate policy changes with long-term cultural shifts that value education and critical thinking, including teacher welfare, training, and curriculum development.

2

Invest in early education, following the model of high-IQ nations that prioritize inclusive, well-prepared early childhood systems.

3

Build job stability through industrial growth, so parents can support their families independently rather than relying on short-term, brokerage-style economic strategies.

4

Reject the framing of national IQ as a verdict on a people's worth, and insist on informed, respectful public dialogue about what the number measures.

Closing

This combination of cultural, educational, and economic investment will help build a foundation for lasting national progress and enhanced cognitive performance — the opposite of what a chimpanzee comparison could ever offer.
