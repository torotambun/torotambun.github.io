Reengineering Culture to
Sustain Prabowo's Leadership

Measured against the 4-Caps+ Framework and the X-Team Model, President Prabowo's leadership shows real strength in sense-making, visioning, relating, and inventing — yet even strong leadership falters when it collides with shared assumptions that prioritize short-term gains and loyalty over long-term collective goals.

Dr. Toronata Tambun
HBS Alumni and Affiliated Alumni of MIT Sloan School of Management.

01  ·  Introduction

Skilled leadership meets an unfit culture.

The framework says the leader is capable. The culture says implementation will lag anyway.

Situation

Leadership and team dynamics are critical to governance and progress, but no leadership framework — global, local, or tailored — can succeed without alignment with a society's cultural foundations.

Complication

In Indonesia, the issue lies not in the leader's skill or preparation but in unfit shared assumptions, as described by Edgar Schein, and misaligned mental models that prioritize short-term gains and loyalty over long-term collective goals, resulting in resistance to change and delayed policy implementation.

Question

Can strong individual leadership overcome cultural barriers that were never designed with that leadership style in mind?

Answer

Not through leadership skill alone — sustaining Prabowo's leadership requires a deliberate, multi-generational reengineering of Indonesia's cultural assumptions, beginning with the family, education, and corporate practice.

02  ·  Gap

A large coalition: inefficiency, or a deliberate experiment?

The same decision reads two ways, depending on what it's tested against.

Core Gap

For many, creating a coalition this large can lead to inefficiencies, lack of accountability, and an overburdened bureaucracy. But this move may reflect a deliberate experiment — one of the hallmarks of inventing as a leadership trait, where leaders take calculated risks and test new models. What's unresolved is whether Indonesia's systemic constraints — bureaucratic inertia, entrenched patronage — will let that experiment prove out before it collapses into the inefficiency critics predict.

03  ·  Framework

The 4-Caps+ Framework, Applied

Sense-making, visioning, relating, and inventing — each strong, each constrained.

01
Sense-Making & Visioning

Targeted energy subsidy reforms show adaptive sense-making; ambitions for industrial growth and regional leadership show visioning — both diluted by coalition politics and competing priorities that favor short-term compromise.

02
Relating

Internationally, Prabowo has excelled at building alliances; domestically, transactional relationships and entrenched patronage hinder deeper collaboration across Indonesia's diverse political and regional stakeholders.

03
Inventing & the Plus Factor

Bold initiatives — free meals for students, wildlife conservation, a Ministry of Human Rights, teacher wage increases — demonstrate goodwill, but credibility depends on whether entrenched corruption and nepotism let those promises become sustained action.

04  ·  Details

Where Team Dynamics Break Down

Scouting, ambassadorship, and task coordination — the X-Team Model's three pressure points.

Scouting: inward-focused, not external

Understanding the organizational terrain and learning from others is often limited as political stakeholders focus inward, neglecting proactive external engagement.

Ambassadorship: fragmented by coalition priorities

Managing relationships with power players and securing strategic alignment is complicated by the fragmented priorities of coalition members, making cohesive action difficult.

Task coordination: hierarchy over collaboration

Clear communication and managing interdependencies struggle against hierarchical structures and transactional relationships that hinder trust and collaborative execution.

06  ·  Implications

Diagnosis: Replace the Culture, or Refine It?

Wholesale cultural replacement is neither realistic nor necessary.

✗ Transitioning to Western Individualism
✗Treating hierarchy and group loyalty themselves as the problem to be eliminated
✗Expecting cultural transformation on the timeline of a single administration
✓ Refining Existing Cultural Strengths
✓Keeping respect for authority and group loyalty while addressing corruption, nepotism, and lack of accountability directly
✓Accepting a 50-to-75-year, two-to-three-generation horizon for cultural transformation, starting with the family unit
✓Embedding accountability and critical thinking through education systems, corporate practice, and civic institutions in parallel

07  ·  Conclusion

A Roadmap Beginning With the Family

Four starting points for a generational cultural reengineering effort.

1

Strengthen family values, dignified employment access, and parents' capacity to support children's education and well-being as the foundational unit of change.

2

Embed ethical, value-driven practice within corporations alongside profitability, driving long-term sustainability rather than short-term extraction.

3

Integrate accountability, inclusivity, critical thinking, and system dynamics thinking into education, preparing future generations for complex governance realities.

4

Adapt leadership style to Indonesia's cultural context, leveraging its strengths while directly addressing counterproductive behaviors.

Closing

These reforms, grounded in Indonesia's unique cultural realities, offer a roadmap to a more accountable, innovative, and equitable society — one where leadership efforts are no longer bound to falter regardless of the framework or individual leading the way.
