Javanese Sayings
for a World Losing Its Way

Four inherited expressions — sarjana tanpa wiyata, ngendika tanpa panggrahita, kuasa tanpa prabawa, meres tanpa deduga — name a condition imported frameworks cannot: not collapse, but drift, where the rituals of a functioning nation remain while the substance beneath them quietly drains.

Dr. Toronata Tambun
HBS Alumni and Affiliated Alumni of MIT Sloan School of Management.
© 2026 Dr. Toronata Tambun
01  ·  Introduction

Signs are abundant. Meaning is scarce.

The outer structure of a functioning nation, with the substance drained out.

Situation

In today's Indonesia, titles are conferred, degrees celebrated, government communication is prolific, and political transitions appear smooth — on the surface, there is motion, sometimes even applause.

Complication

For those living inside the system, those structures often feel vacant. Performance abounds, but depth is thin. The rituals remain, yet the substance beneath them has quietly drained.

Question

What language can name a crisis that is not collapse but drift, not absence but substitution?

Answer

Four inherited Javanese expressions — on credentials, speech, power, and extraction — capture patterns of erosion more precisely than imported frameworks, and point to a generational, not cosmetic, repair.

02  ·  Gap

Chronic depletion, not collapse

Society adapts to lowered expectations without noticing the adaptation.

Core Gap

Degrees lose their credibility. Public speech loses its meaning. Political leadership loses its independence. Economic participation loses its dignity. Institutions remain but become less able to serve. The appearance of function continues — but the core no longer holds. This is not collapse, but chronic depletion. And when depletion becomes normal, society adapts to lowered expectations.

03  ·  Framework

Four Javanese Diagnoses

Each names a distinct domain of the same erosion.

01
Credentials
Sarjana tanpa wiyata

Graduates without learning — when academic titles become social capital rather than intellectual contribution, appearance becomes enough and merit can be simulated.

02
Speech
Ngendika tanpa panggrahita

Speaking without thought — official language that forgets meaning is never neutral, turning communication into performance rather than connection.

03
Power
Kuasa tanpa prabawa

Authority without traction — titles held while mandates are diluted, legitimacy borrowed rather than built, influence invoked rather than exercised.

04
Extraction
Meres tanpa deduga

Extraction without foresight — platform efficiency whose burden falls on MSMEs and informal sellers rather than on the systems that profit from it.

04  ·  Details

Where Each Pattern Shows Up

Concrete instances behind each of the four sayings.

Sarjana Tanpa Wiyata in Practice

Advanced degrees proliferating among political figures, often obtained through unclear paths with little trace of intellectual labor, hollowing out public trust in academic institutions.

Ngendika Tanpa Panggrahita in Practice

The controversy over terms like rakyat jelata, ndasmu, and kamu gelap was never about definitions — citing a dictionary misses that meaning is relational and political, as McLuhan and Derrida both show.

Kuasa Tanpa Prabawa in Practice

A president's public invocation of his predecessor reveals how legitimacy today is borrowed rather than built — the repetition of names substitutes for governance.

Meres Tanpa Deduga in Practice

Platforms that operate at a loss, fueled by investor speculation, while small producers forced into races to the bottom are left unprotected — organized precarity, not market efficiency.

05  ·  Evidence

Grounding the Argument

Media theory, philosophy, and the author's own neurodevelopment research.

EVIDENCE A
McLuhan — "the medium is the message"

Applied to official Indonesian discourse, explaining why a spokesperson's dictionary defense of contested language misses how words from authority carry weight beyond semantics.

EVIDENCE B
Derrida on context, iterability, and hierarchy

Underpins the claim that meaning is never neutral and communication is relational, situated, and political rather than technical.

EVIDENCE C
A scene from Schindler's List

Oskar Schindler's remark to Amon Göth that real power lies in restraint, not the act, illustrates the moral traction missing from Indonesian public authority.

EVIDENCE D
The author's own neurodevelopment research

On neural pruning, synaptic reinforcement, and memory encoding in early childhood, grounding the claim that repair must begin in the first decade of life.

06  ·  Implications

Diagnosis: Display or Depth?

Only one survives contact with consequence.

✗ Prioritizing Display
✗Treating credentials, communication, and legitimacy as things to perform rather than earn
✗Accepting platform-driven "efficiency" without asking who actually absorbs its cost
✓ Rebuilding Depth
✓Testing what looks like progress for substance, and weighing what is celebrated for consequence
✓Investing in early childhood moral formation as the root-level foundation for institutions
✓Committing to a 50–75-year, two-to-three-generation horizon rather than a policy cycle
© 2026 Dr. Toronata Tambun
07  ·  Conclusion

Beginning Again at the Root

Repair is generational, and it starts before adulthood.

1

Rebuild institutions from the foundation of early moral formation, not from the top down through policy alone.

2

Commit to a 50 to 75-year horizon of persistent investment in public ethics, spanning two to three generations.

3

Undertake a civic and cultural reckoning — a national acknowledgment that display has been prioritized over depth, and a turn back toward substance.

Closing

Without that turn — without the choice to begin again at the root — what survives may still resemble a functioning nation but will no longer be able to renew itself.
