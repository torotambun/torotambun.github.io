A self-note before the defense: On generalizability, positivist reflexes, and a basic misunderstanding of auto-ethnography

Generalizability, reliability, repeatability and confidence form a coherent checklist inside positivism and a category error outside it. This dissertation does not reject rigour; it rejects the assumption that positivist validity criteria are the only legitimate ones. Written before the defense, to itself: the task is not to correct examiners but to stay aligned.

Dr. Toronata Tambun
Director, Yayasan Mens et Manus
© 2026 Dr. Toronata Tambun
Opening

One recurring question exposes a misunderstanding rather than a substantive critique.

A note written to himself, before the defense, and published as it stands.

Situation

"As the defense approaches, several familiar questions surface. Most are legitimate. Some are revealing."

Complication

One in particular deserves clarity, because "it exposes a recurring misunderstanding rather than a substantive critique." "Generalizability, reliability, repeatability, confidence. These are often raised together, as if they form a universal checklist for what counts as scientific work."

Question

If those four criteria are not the universal test they are treated as, what exactly is the standard this dissertation should be held to instead?

Answer

Different criteria, and the same demand for discipline. The dissertation is grounded in autoethnography and qualitative system dynamics. "It does not reject rigor. It rejects the assumption that positivist validity criteria are the only legitimate ones."

The Checklist

Inside positivism the checklist is coherent; it is not therefore universal.

"To remain precise under pressure, the distinctions must be explicit."

"In positivist traditions, they do. That system is internally coherent." Statistical generalizability, instrument reliability, replicability, degrees of confidence and prediction are tightly coupled — remove one, and the claim weakens. The checklist earns its authority honestly, within its own paradigm.

"But that system is not universal." The assumptions that make it coherent are assumptions, not defaults. "They are not neutral. They presuppose a certain kind of data, distance, and epistemic stance."

That is the whole of the distinction the note is trying to hold. Not that the positivist criteria are wrong, but that they are local — and that treating a local standard as a universal one is what produces the question worth answering carefully.

The Category Error

Autoethnography is primary-source research, and that is definitional, not opinion.

"Autoethnography does not begin with samples, instruments, or detached observers. It begins with situated lived experience, examined reflexively and analytically."

What the Positivist Reflex Expects
✗A sample, drawn to support statistical generalization
✗An instrument, whose reliability can be measured
✗A detached observer, standing at epistemic distance
✗A study assembled from secondary sources
✗Validity established by replication
What Autoethnography Actually Is
✓"Autoethnography is, by definition, primary-source research."
✓"The primary data are lived experience, systematically documented, reflexively analyzed, and theoretically situated."
✓"Secondary sources may contextualize, frame, or dialogue with that experience, but they do not replace it."
✓It does not read from secondary sources: "It reflects on experience, then situates that reflection analytically."
✓"Different criteria. Same demand for discipline."
How the note treats the comment it is answering

One examiner's comment suggested the study might rely on “secondary sources”. The note does not treat that as an attack: "That comment is not a criticism. It is a misunderstanding." "Applying positivist criteria mechanically to autoethnography is not rigor. It is a category error." "It is akin to asking an ethnographer where their survey instrument is." "This is not a matter of opinion. It is definitional."

Where Generalization Operates

Generalization operates at the level of structure, not population.

"This study does not claim statistical generalizability." That position follows directly from its autoethnographic and qualitative system dynamics design.

It is a design consequence, not a concession extracted under questioning. The claim being made is narrower than the positivist one and is not a weaker version of it.

"The question is not “does this apply everywhere”." That is the question the checklist is built to answer, and it is the wrong one to put to this study.

"The question is “does this mechanism exist, and under what conditions”." A feedback structure that is shown to operate, with its boundaries stated, generalizes analytically and theoretically — which is a different kind of claim, held to a different kind of test.

Triangulation

Triangulation is non-negotiable, and it tests structure rather than manufacturing generalizability.

The note concedes the point a sceptic would press hardest, and then closes it.

"Autoethnography without triangulation deserves skepticism. This dissertation therefore treats triangulation as non-negotiable." The scepticism is granted rather than deflected, which is what makes the requirement binding instead of decorative.

But its function has to be stated precisely. "Triangulation here does not manufacture generalizability." It is not a route back to the criteria the study has just declined.

"It tests whether structure survives multiple lenses." That is a test the study can fail, which is the point of having it.

The sentence to hold steady in the defense

"This study does not claim statistical generalizability. Rigor is established through reflexive transparency and explicit triangulation, enabling analytic and theoretical generalization at the level of feedback structures within clearly defined contextual boundaries." The note adds only: "That sentence is sufficient."

Final Self-Reminder

The task is not to correct examiners but to remain aligned and unprovoked.

The note ends by addressing its own author rather than its readers.

Alignment beats argument.

— A self-note before the defense
Sorting the questions before they arrive
"Not every critique is methodological. Some are epistemic. Not every question is deep. Some reveal shallow familiarity with the method." The sorting is done in advance precisely so that it does not have to be done under pressure.
What the note refuses to do
"The task in the defense is not to correct examiners. It is to remain aligned, precise, and unprovoked." Being right about the method and winning the exchange about it are separate objectives, and only one of them is the task.
Where the legitimacy comes from
"Autoethnography does not borrow legitimacy from positivism. It stands on its own criteria." That is the claim the whole note exists to make sayable in one breath.
