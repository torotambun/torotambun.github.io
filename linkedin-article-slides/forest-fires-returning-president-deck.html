Why Forest Fires Keep Returning to the President

The fires recur for reasons of plantation economics, peat drainage and weather. They also recur because institutions treat an instruction issued as a responsibility discharged. Where recognition attaches to the identifiable activity rather than to the condition of the land, prevention is the work that is never assessed, and the same emergency returns.

Dr. Toronata Tambun
Director, Yayasan Mens et Manus
© 2026 Dr. Toronata Tambun
Introduction

The habitat is demonstrably under threat, which is reason enough for concern without unverified claims attached to photographs.

"The sadness surrounding the fires in Sumatra and Kalimantan is difficult to separate from the knowledge that orangutans depend on the affected forests for food and shelter."

Situation

On 13 August 2026 the Borneo Orangutan Survival Foundation "reported that more than 43 hectares within its Mawas conservation landscape had already been burned and affected". Its appeal described the threat alongside "the patrols, water management, equipment, and community cooperation needed to prevent further damage".

Complication

The evidence is bounded, and the article says so. "The report does not establish a current count of orangutans dying or suffering hunger. It does establish that the habitat supporting their survival is under threat."

Question

By 26 August the ASEAN Specialised Meteorological Centre had activated Alert Level 3, reporting haze "drifting northward from West Kalimantan", so that "People beyond the burned areas were consequently exposed to harm arising from land they neither owned nor managed". Why does this keep happening?

Answer

"Plantation development, peat drainage, weather, and enforcement belong in the explanation, but so does the way institutions determine whether their responsibilities have been fulfilled."

The Decision

Burning is a land-management choice rather than a requirement of oil-palm cultivation, and has been documented as such since 2006.

"Burning can reduce the immediate expense of clearing land."

CIFOR "documented its use by plantation companies, smallholders, and other land users in 2006, while also identifying fires associated with access to resources and land disputes".

Its recommendations "included support for alternatives to burning, showing that fire was understood as a land-management choice rather than a requirement of oil-palm cultivation".

That framing matters for attribution. A practice that is chosen can be changed, and the absence of change becomes a question for whoever is responsible for the alternatives.

© 2026 Dr. Toronata Tambun
The Calculation

The cost advantage survives only because the losses fall on people who are not doing the calculating.

"The cost advantage depends on what enters the calculation."

Inside the land user's calculation
•Clearing expenses avoided
•The immediate cost of the method to the user
•A boundary that appears to contain the decision
Outside it, and borne by others
✗"surrounding communities bear losses from smoke, disrupted work, and damaged resources"
✗Effects "on health, agricultural production, schooling, and public resources diverted to firefighting" (CIFOR, 2006)
✗"Judging the method only by its immediate cost to the user would exclude these consequences"
© 2026 Dr. Toronata Tambun
Peat

Drainage inside a concession raises the fire risk outside it, so the property boundary cannot contain the responsibility.

"Peat drainage makes the relationship between private decisions and wider harm particularly important."

"Drained and degraded peat becomes susceptible to fire, while the resulting smoke can affect neighbouring countries" (Kopansky, 2017).

"Research on Indonesian concessions found that drainage within a concession could increase fire vulnerability outside it. Fires could also spread across boundaries that did not always correspond with actual land occupation" (Gaveau et al., 2017).

So a hotspot location is not an answer. An investigation "must establish who controlled the land, where ignition occurred, and whether surrounding management practices contributed to the spread". Gaveau et al. found "independent farmers occupying some concession land and companies operating beyond registered boundaries, making field investigation necessary for attributing responsibility".

Why naming the igniter is not enough

"Identifying whoever started a fire may leave unanswered the earlier decisions that made it difficult to contain."

The Instruction

Presidential attention has been explicit since 2016, which makes recurrence a question about implementation rather than awareness.

The obligations were assigned years before this season, and that is what shifts the burden of assessment.

Sep 2019
Prevention named as the cheaper path
During a visit to a fire site in Riau, President Joko Widodo "emphasized that prevention was more effective and less expensive than extinguishing established fires".
Feb 2020
The meeting is already a routine
He "described the coordination meeting as an annual routine held since 2016".
Mar 2020
Presidential Instruction No. 3 of 2020
It "addressed prevention, suppression, recovery, enforcement, and coordination across national and local institutions".
2026
The assessment that follows
"Because these obligations were already assigned, assessing a subsequent intervention requires following what happened to the preceding instructions."

Authority

An instruction proves the power of office was exercised; only implementation proves it reached the ground.

"The distinction between formal authority and practical influence becomes relevant at this point."

"An instruction demonstrates that someone has exercised the power of office. Evidence that the instruction changed field conditions must come from implementation."

The failure mode is a public one. "When the first is accepted as proof of the second, public assessment stops before the consequences have been examined."

"The condition described as kuasa tanpa prabawa concerns this separation between position and practical effect" (Tambun, 2025). The operative test is "whether the authority exercised through meetings and instructions reaches the maintenance of peatland conditions, the readiness of local teams, and the completion of investigations".

The claim the article declines to make

"Presidential involvement may be necessary to mobilize resources during a serious emergency. Its recurrence does not prove that local institutions are inactive." What it does make appropriate is scrutiny of continuity: "whether responsibilities remain funded and operational when senior attention moves elsewhere".

© 2026 Dr. Toronata Tambun
Displacement

Where recognition attaches to the identifiable activity, prevention becomes the work nobody has to demonstrate.

"The way institutions recognize achievement can affect that continuity."

The asymmetry is structural. "A meeting, deployment, or inspection is an identifiable activity. Demonstrating successful prevention requires following conditions over time and examining whether the work reduced vulnerability."

"If recognition rests mainly on completed activities, officials may receive credit for a response without an equivalent examination of preparation." It extends the argument about performative innovation, "where titles, affiliations, and ceremonies can carry more weight than what an initiative actually produces" (Tambun, 2026).

The imbalance is not new. CIFOR's 2006 diagnosis "identified an emphasis on suppression that diverted resources from prevention and underlying causes. It also identified unclear institutional responsibilities and weak judicial follow-through".

"The cultural question concerns how a fulfilled obligation is understood. If producing a report or carrying out a deployment is sufficient, unresolved vulnerability can remain outside the assessment."

What the sources do not settle

The later presidential emphasis on prevention "shows that the need was recognized, although the sources assembled here do not establish how far those earlier weaknesses have since been corrected".

And what is not being alleged

"This does not require assuming that Indonesians are indifferent to forests." BOS Foundation's account "documents sustained work by staff and communities, including patrols and water management".

© 2026 Dr. Toronata Tambun
Empathy

A report can record personnel and equipment while saying little about whether anyone received protection.

"The consequences experienced by affected communities provide another way to evaluate institutional activity."

"A report may record personnel and equipment deployed while saying little about whether people received the protection or assistance they needed. Those operational figures remain useful, but their meaning depends on the conditions they were intended to improve."

An earlier reflection on crisis leadership "identified honesty, a rational basis for hope, and authentic empathy as expectations of leaders" (Tambun, 2020). In a recurring emergency, "honesty includes an account of unresolved risks and incomplete obligations".

The rakyat jelata column "concerned the responsibility of officials to understand how words reach people from a position of authority" (Tambun, 2024). "That responsibility extends to explaining a response in terms meaningful to those living with its consequences."

And the harm does not stop at the border. Smoke crossing national boundaries was documented in the August alert, so "The location of the fire does not confine the responsibility to understand its effects".

The Same Standard

Affiliation is not an argument, and the test applied to officials has to be applied to educated circles as well.

"The dependence on visible standing also deserves examination within professional and alumni networks." A recent appeal to speak "as intellectuals under the MIT name" expressed concern about the fires, "but introduced affiliation as part of the justification for speaking".

What a name cannot do
✗"An institutional name can indicate access to education and networks"
✗"It does not establish the relevance or quality of a particular contribution"
✗Allowed to supply that judgment in advance, it "resembles the substitution of status for demonstrated results criticized elsewhere"
✗The same standard "should apply when examining officials and ourselves"
What can actually be assessed
✓"the evidence offered"
✓"the expertise relevant to the problem"
✓"the work undertaken"
✓Participation "proportionate to what is available without requiring a declaration of intellectual distinction"
Two diagnoses explicitly refused

"This does not support a judgment that MIT alumni as a group are insensitive." And on the reply that it has nothing to do with me: "the sources discussed here do not establish that middle-class instability causes diminished empathy. Treating that connection as a finding would turn a plausible question into an unsupported diagnosis."

Implications

Five matters can be followed past the emergency, and reporting them against earlier commitments is what would make prevention visible.

"The evidence identifies several matters that can be followed beyond the emergency" (CIFOR, 2006; Gaveau et al., 2017; Kopansky, 2017).

1
The management of vulnerable peat
The condition that converts a local clearing decision into transboundary harm, and the one that persists between seasons.
2
Support for alternatives to burning
CIFOR's own recommendation, and the test of whether fire is still being treated as a choice that can be changed.
3
Local preparedness
Whether the people carrying preventive responsibilities "receive dependable support" once senior attention moves elsewhere.
4
Investigation of land control and ignition
Establishing who controlled the land and whether surrounding practices contributed to the spread, which requires field investigation rather than hotspot mapping.

Public reporting would be more informative if it connected these matters with earlier commitments. That would allow an assessment of completed work, remaining risks, and the institutions responsible for them.

Conclusion

Following the work across seasons is what would turn concern into a practical direction.

"The sadness about orangutans belongs within this assessment because their survival depends on the forest remaining capable of feeding and sheltering them." BOS Foundation's account "describes work that must continue across seasons, regardless of whether the fires are receiving national attention".

It would also bring the discussion of authority back to the consequences of its exercise, where the condition of the forest matters more than the prominence of the person announcing its protection.
