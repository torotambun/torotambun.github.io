Indonesia's Brightest Financial Minds Explain the Stock Market Crash. Surely, They Have Done the Causal Analysis.

MSCI’s October 2026 consultation is a defensible immediate trigger for the crash, but not a complete explanation: bank prices and foreign selling had weakened long before it. The Danantara dividend channel is a serious, testable hypothesis, yet BCA’s parallel fall and an illustrative model whose ranking follows its assumptions show that only a dated panel with an identification strategy can apportion cause.

Dr. Toronata Tambun
Director, Yayasan Mens et Manus
© 2026 Dr. Toronata Tambun
Introduction

MSCI can be named the trigger of the October crash, but not the complete explanation of a decline that began earlier.

"Someone on the government side once reminded me that Indonesia has many people smarter than me." "I have never disputed that proposition."

Situation

On 21 October 2026 the OJK Chairperson addressed the cabinet on MSCI’s warning and the regulatory response. "Her remarks provide the occasion for this inquiry; the causal questions that follow are mine".

Complication

"Major banks had suffered substantial valuation losses before MSCI’s October announcement, and foreign investors had been selling Indonesian equities during 2025."

Question

"What drove the preceding retreat, and why did the October announcement provoke a market-wide selloff?"

Answer

Separate the immediate trigger from the domestic conditions that preceded it. "This is a documentary investigation, and my concern about state capital allocation remains a hypothesis."

Evidence

The big four banks had already fallen from their 2024 highs by May 2026, and foreign investors had sold heavily through 2025.

"The first problem with treating MSCI's October announcement as a complete explanation is chronology."

RECORD A
Prices before October
Katadata (May 2026) documented Mandiri, BRI, BCA and BNI against their 2024 highs, using rounded, non-matched reference prices. The January 2026 MSCI freeze was already in place.
RECORD B
Foreign selling in 2025
MNC Sekuritas Exhibit 52: net foreign selling of Rp28.204 trillion in BBCA and Rp13.336 trillion in BMRI in calendar 2025. "the underlying Bloomberg transactions were not independently audited."
RECORD C
The month around the crash
IDNFinancials recorded Rp14.5 trillion of net foreign selling from 17 September to 16 October 2026. The window includes the crash day, so it "cannot be treated as an entirely pre-announcement measurement."
RECORD D
Before Danantara existed
"The decline had already begun before Danantara formally existed." Expectations of a future announcement remain "a separate, testable channel."
Date is not motive

"The causal analysis must distinguish the date of selling from the motives for selling."

© 2026 Dr. Toronata Tambun
Details

Mandiri paid out 79% and BRI about 92% of 2025 profit while lending grew, which makes capital retention a testable question.

S&P projected SOE dividends of about US$5 billion to US$6 billion a year as Danantara’s principal funding source. "This was a funding forecast, not a record of realized receipts."

DISCLOSED A
Bank Mandiri
Rp44.47 trillion in dividends from 2025 profit of Rp56.3 trillion, a reported 79% payout, while lending grew 13.4% to about Rp1,895 trillion.
DISCLOSED B
The counterfactual
A 50% policy would have retained about Rp16.32 trillion more. "This does not mean that Rp16.32 trillion in additional capital would automatically have remained available for lending."
DISCLOSED C
BRI
Rp52.102 trillion distributed against Rp56.652 trillion owners’ profit, 91.97%. Group loans up 16.2% while CAR fell from about 25% to 21.5%.
DISCLOSED D
What the CAR fall means
"it is a 14% reduction in the ratio, not evidence that the amount of regulatory capital fell 14%." Profit improved and gross NPL declined.
The bank’s own assessment

Mandiri stated the September interim dividend would not materially affect its operations or capital. "That assessment belongs beside the concern about retention." Dividends are "not, by itself, evidence of wrongdoing or value destruction."

Gap

Privately controlled BCA fell alongside the state banks, so a Danantara-only explanation fails the private-bank comparison.

"BCA is essential because it tests whether the decline can be explained solely by state ownership."

What BCA shows
✓FY2025 bank-only CAR of 29.8% and gross NPL of 1.7%, yet substantial share-price deterioration
✓On 16 October: Mandiri −14.82%, BRI −14.84%, BCA −14.81%, a rounded spread of 0.03 points
✓"consistent with a broadly shared market shock on that day."
✓A Danantara effect "must be identified against the common decline affecting private and state-controlled banks."
What BCA cannot show
✗"BCA is not an untreated experimental control: its business model differs, and state policy can also affect private-bank expectations."
✗"A common decline cannot prove that Danantara had no market-wide spillover."
✗Similar rounded returns "do not independently identify a common causal mechanism."
✗"The longer valuation decline is a different matter."
© 2026 Dr. Toronata Tambun
Evidence

MSCI warned in January and July before proposing a reclassification consultation, and the JCI fell 11.57% on the Jakarta trading day that followed.

"MSCI’s October announcement did not emerge without prior warning." Dates in Jakarta time.

Jan 2026
Freeze on index changes
27 January: a freeze on specified index changes.
Jul 2026
Restrictions continued
6 July: restrictions on Indonesian securities continued.
Oct 2026
Consultation, then the crash
16 October, 03:40 WIB: consultation on moving Indonesia from Emerging Market to Standalone Market status, citing investability and governance concerns. The JCI fell 11.57% that trading day.
Jun 2027
Decision due
Feedback closes 30 April 2027 and a decision is due 30 June 2027. "The proposal was not a completed reclassification."
Attributed, not adjudicated

"Suspected coordinated trading remains MSCI’s reported concern, not a finding adjudicated in this article."

© 2026 Dr. Toronata Tambun
Details

The Bayan block trade raises ownership and price-discovery questions but has no documentary link to MSCI’s October consultation.

Jhonlin Baratama reportedly bought about 30.25% of Bayan on 7 October at Rp2,588 a share; a small mid-October purchase by Low Tuck Kwong was reported at Rp11,200.

The gap is (11,200 − 2,588) / 11,200 × 100 = 76.9%, but "The prices concern different dates, quantities and transaction types." It "does not automatically represent a loss to Bayan's operating assets or demonstrate manipulation." The central questions are economic substance, beneficial ownership, voting control and the basis for the price.

"Bayan’s October transaction could not have caused MSCI’s earlier January and July notices." "There is no documentary basis at present for concluding that Bayan's transaction directly triggered MSCI's October consultation."

What was not inspected

"The underlying response letter was not independently inspected here. These are attributed news accounts, not a finding about the identity, motives or legality of the ultimate controller."

Framework

A proposed PLS-SEM design separates three hypothesised drivers, asking through mediation how pressure travels and through moderation when it intensifies.

"Arrows are hypotheses, not estimates."

01
Underlying
State capital-allocation pressure
Dividend distributions, retained earnings, directed lending and the possible influence of Danantara’s investment-funding requirements.
02
Underlying
Market-governance weaknesses
Concentrated shareholding, ownership disclosure problems, suspected coordinated trading and the credibility of enforcement.
03
Shock
MSCI intervention
Dated announcements, index-eligibility changes and the anticipated effect on international institutional investors.
Not a clean experiment

"MSCI’s assessment can itself respond to domestic governance, so it is not assumed to be randomly exogenous." "reverse causality and feedback remain possible."

The fundamental distinction

"An announcement may have a significant effect on certain investors without being the original source of the weakness that made those investors withdraw."

© 2026 Dr. Toronata Tambun
Details

Assumed coefficients give state pressure a total of 0.571 against MSCI’s 0.355, which shows only that the assumptions set the ranking.

"It is not an empirical PLS-SEM estimate; it uses no observed or synthetic dataset. Its nine path coefficients are assigned assumptions."

State pressure: 0.10 + 0.29575 + 0.17550 = 0.57125. MSCI: 0.08 + 0.275 = 0.355. With foreign exposure as moderator, the conditional MSCI totals are 0.295, 0.355 and 0.415 at Z = −1, 0 and +1.

"A model that assigns larger coefficients to state-related pathways will naturally produce a larger combined effect for those pathways." "The same would be true of a model constructed to favor MSCI." The totals "must not be normalized into causal shares", and the governance pathway is not quantified.

What cannot be claimed

"No empirical confidence intervals, reliability statistics, bootstrap significance, model fit, predictive accuracy or causal effect estimates can legitimately be claimed for this exercise."

© 2026 Dr. Toronata Tambun
Implications

A real causal study needs a dated bank panel, comparative and event-study designs, and an identification strategy before any cause is ranked.

"Repeating an annual observation across many rows does not create independent evidence."

1  ·  A harmonised panel
Time-series and bank-level observations from 2024 through October 2026, reconciling annual dividends, quarterly balance sheets and daily returns.
2  ·  Decision-level attribution
"It would be inaccurate to attribute every dividend decision to Danantara without evidence of its role in the specific decision."
3  ·  Comparative and event designs
Difference-in-differences or a comparative panel of bank valuations, with event studies around MSCI announcements, parallel trends and checks for anticipation and spillovers.
4  ·  A defensible measurement model
PLS-SEM only where data support it: justified reflective or formative constructs, sufficient independent observations and attention to endogeneity.
© 2026 Dr. Toronata Tambun
Conclusion

MSCI as immediate trigger is defensible, while MSCI as complete explanation is not, until the authorities publish their causal decomposition.

"It does not establish that Friderica personally claimed MSCI was the sole cause of every preceding market loss. Without the video transcripts, I cannot responsibly assign that stronger claim to her or to a particular broadcaster."

Until such analysis becomes available, identifying MSCI as the immediate trigger is defensible. Treating its announcement as a complete explanation of Indonesia's preceding financial deterioration is not.
