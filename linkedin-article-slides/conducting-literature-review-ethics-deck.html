Conducting Literature Review and Ethical Studies

The article is a summary of Neuman's chapter on the literature review, and what makes it more than a summary is the order in which it happened: the author read the chapter after submitting his own State of the Art, so the reading became an audit of finished work rather than a guide to unstarted work. Neuman's six types give him a name for what his SOTA is, the self-study type becomes a review template of his own, and the criterion he brings to the whole exercise is the one from the McEnerney piece he published the same afternoon — see through the reader's eyes.

Opening

The book arrived from Amazon in four days and took me back to a Wisconsin lecture hall.

The article opens on the physical object, and the feeling it produced, before any method appears.

"As an old-timer, my mind quickly expands, reminiscent of being transported to a Wisconsin lecture hall, the very place where Neuman's book was crafted."

The prompt was a class, and the delivery was fast: "The touch of the book, prompted by Prof. Gatot Yudoko 's Quantitative Methodology Class, ignites a swift journey to the classroom." The book "only took four days to arrive from Amazon" and "evoked the heartwarming anticipation I felt as a child, waiting for my parents to return from work."

What he did with it was deliberately unhurried: "Immersing myself in its chapters, I found myself engrossed, contemplating an all-nighter with the literature. But instead, I chose to reflect and digest the material."

And the reason he could afford to: "This isn't for a grade; my State of the Art (SOTA) document was joyfully submitted in January, even though a two-week overhaul was needed."

The debt the article records

The overhaul "however rigorous, was made enjoyable thanks to the discipline instilled in me, for which I must thank Dr. Leo Aldianto, my co-promotor, who pushed for a fresh start." A fresh start on a document already written is the hardest instruction a supervisor gives.

The Turn

I read the chapter on conducting a literature review after I had already submitted mine.

This is the sentence that makes the piece an argument rather than a set of notes.

The reading becomes retrospective assessment: "Summarizing Chapter 5, the literature review chapter, I found myself in a reflective moment, pondering on what could have been improved."

And the criterion he judges it by comes from his own teaching, not from Neuman: "Integrating this with my communication class on 'writing,' I emphasized the importance of seeing through the reader's eyes."

That criterion is the argument of the McEnerney article he published seven hours later the same day — that value in academic writing is conferred by the reader's community rather than earned by the writer's effort. The two pieces are one thought, read from two directions.

The article's cover caption names what the whole exercise is for: finding joy in reading a physical book.

Framework

Neuman's six types let me name what my State of the Art actually is.

The six types of literature review as the source slides set them out, all credited to Neuman's Chapter 5.

1  ·  Context review
Linking a study to broader knowledge, introducing the study within a larger framework, and building on developing thought or study.
2  ·  Historical review
Tracing an issue's evolution over time, combining with theoretical and methodological review, and developing concepts, theories or methods over time.
3  ·  Integrative review
Summarising a state of knowledge, highlighting agreements and disagreements, and standing as an independent article that is a service to other researchers. This is the type the author's own SOTA belongs to, and the slide carrying his December 2023 submission is captioned as an integrative review.
4  ·  Methodological review
Comparing methodological strengths, examining different methodologies and their varying results, and assessing methodological impacts on findings.
5  ·  Self-study review
Demonstrating an author's familiarity with a subject, forming part of an educational or course requirement, and demonstrating knowledge in an area.
6  ·  Theoretical review
Presenting and comparing theories, examining assumptions, logical consistency and explanation scope, and focusing on theory development and comparison.
What a literature review is for, on Neuman's account

The four goals given in the slides are demonstrating familiarity with a body of knowledge, showing the path of prior research and its relevance, integrating and summarising what is known, and learning from others while stimulating new ideas.

Details

The self-study type became a three-block review template of my own.

The one slide in the set that is not Neuman's — the author's own template for reviewing a journal article. Its wording does not survive text extraction and is transcribed here from a rendered page of the source, so it is given unquoted.

01
Article being reviewed
The slide asks for a brief title reflecting the main idea of the review. The block that forces the reviewer to state what the article is about before saying anything about it.
02
Critical evaluation
The slide names this section the heart of the review, and asks the reviewer to discuss the strengths and weaknesses of the article. Named as the heart of the exercise, and placed in the middle.
03
Personal reflection
The slide describes a more subjective section covering what was learned from the article, what questions it raised, and how it might influence the reviewer's own work or thinking — including comparing the journal against one's own dissertation chapter and quoting it in the review where relevant.
Why the third block is the useful one

The first two blocks describe the article. Only the third connects it to the reader's own work in progress, which is the mechanism by which reading becomes revision — the same mechanism the whole piece is an instance of.

Field Notes

Where to find literature, and what ethics has to do with a reading chapter.

The remaining slides cover sources and then turn, in the chapter's own structure, to research ethics.

The publication types are set out as scholarly journals, books, dissertations, government documents, and policy reports and presented papers — each with its own characteristics and its own standing as evidence.

The chapter then moves to ethics in social research, and the framing given on the slide is a tension rather than a checklist: the importance of ethical considerations is presented as balancing scientific knowledge pursuit against participant rights.

That pairing is why the article's title carries both halves. A literature review and a research-ethics review are the two obligations a researcher discharges before the first datum is collected, and Neuman's chapter treats them together.

The article closes with a wish list rather than a conclusion: a comparison table of reviewed manuscripts, especially in how they address the research questions, credited to a classmate's handout.

Conclusion

Reading it afterwards taught me to see through the reader's eyes.

The article's own closing thought, which is the only claim in it that is not Neuman's.

Integrating this with my communication class on 'writing,' I emphasized the importance of seeing through the reader's eyes.

— Conducting Literature Review and Ethical Studies, 10 March 2024
Why the order matters
A methods chapter read before writing is instruction. The same chapter read after submission is an audit, and it produces a different kind of knowledge — not what to do, but what was missed. The article is a record of the second kind.
What was already done well
The SOTA was submitted in January and the two-week overhaul had already happened, prompted by a co-promotor who asked for a fresh start. The reflection is not regret; it is an experienced writer checking finished work against a standard he met afterwards.
Where the standard came from
Not from Neuman, whose chapter is about method, but from the author's own communication teaching and from McEnerney — whose handout is the first entry in this article's reference list, and whose argument the author published in full the same afternoon.
