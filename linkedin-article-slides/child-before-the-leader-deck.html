The Child Before the Leader

Early experience does not determine destiny; it builds the architecture through which later experience is interpreted. Because the largest theoretical weight sits before age seven while public urgency arrives long afterwards, Indonesia's problem is not a shortage of programmes or knowledge but a misallocation of attention.

Dr. Toronata Tambun
Director, Yayasan Mens et Manus
© 2026 Dr. Toronata Tambun
Opening

A boy asleep beside his mother's cart, a few kilometres from the Palace, is already being taught what the state is.

"I first remember Rosyid as a baby being breastfed beside his mother’s tahu goreng cart in Menteng."

Situation

Years later he is in primary school. "Because there is nobody at home to look after him after school, his father brings him to the cart." He has been seen there "with a tablet, beneath a tarpaulin when it rains, and late at night asleep on the pavement beside his mother". "The cart stands only a few kilometres from the Palace."

Complication

"This is not a sentimental opening about poverty. It is a question about national vision." Which circuits are being exercised repeatedly, and which "expectations about safety, attention, work, strangers, technology, fairness and the state are becoming familiar before he has the language to explain them?"

Question

"And why does government usually become most visible much later—when it wants his examination scores, employability, obedience, productivity or vote?"

Answer

The archive leads to a proposition: "we spend too much time trying to repair the adult and too little time understanding the child who preceded the adult".

The Claim

Early formation carries the largest weight in the model, and declared character the smallest.

The peer-reviewed paper assigns approximate explanatory weights of "45 percent to early neural pathways, 22 percent to cognitive biases, 18 percent to external pressures, 10 percent to context and 5 percent to individual character".

"The hierarchy is the argument. Early formation receives the largest weight; bias is second; pressure and context remain powerful; consciously declared character is the smallest component."

That ordering is what unsettles the usual account. "This challenges the comfortable assumption that a good adult is simply someone who “has character.”" An executive "may sincerely value honesty yet rationalize a small exception, follow a compromised group, obey an authority, respond to a threat or gradually accept conduct that would once have felt wrong".

"Research on ethical fading, gradual moral erosion and organizational misconduct explains why neither intention nor a values poster is enough."

The limit stated by the author, not by a critic

"The 45 percent is not a biomarker, a national statistic or the result of following children for decades." The paper is "a conceptual narrative literature review" whose weights are "a disciplined theoretical allocation, not an experimentally estimated population variance".

The Second Limit

Biography can generate patterns, but it cannot show the synapse that produced an adult decision.

"Intellectual honesty requires one more limit. Biography cannot prove a neural mechanism."

A remembered kitchen or childhood humiliation "may reveal a plausible continuity. It cannot show us the synapse that produced an adult decision."

The distortions are named rather than waved away. "Retrospective stories are selective; successful people are overrepresented; narrators search for coherence; observers, including me, bring theories to what they hear."

So the evidential status is fixed in advance. The field notes "should therefore be read as pattern-generating evidence placed beside developmental science—not as a substitute for longitudinal research".

The term that replaces fate

"The scientific term to emphasize is therefore not fate but developmental probability." It "neither creates an unchangeable adult nor absolves the adult of responsibility".

Development

Formation begins long before the classroom, and the units have to be kept distinct to keep the claim honest.

"Yet three is not the true beginning." From pregnancy through the first three years at least five processes run together, and the brain is "constructing, selecting, reorganizing and improving communication across networks at the same time".

1  ·  Pregnancy to three
"Across much of the period, formation outpaces elimination, producing net expansion, but sensory and association regions follow different schedules." Synaptic density peaked "at about three months in auditory cortex but not until after fifteen months in middle frontal cortex".
2  ·  Three to seven
High turnover with increasingly complex social learning. Genes associated with synaptic function in the prefrontal cortex "peaked around age five". That is "important evidence for prolonged prefrontal synaptic development", and "not a measurement of whole-brain neuronal firing".
3  ·  Eight to seventeen, then seventeen to twenty-four
"Pruning did not suddenly begin at eight. What changes is the balance." The later band is "an interpretive extension in my ongoing postdoctoral work, not an exact age boundary established by that paper".
4  ·  Then adult institutions
They "activate, reward, constrain or corrupt what is available. Pressure does not merely reveal a fixed character; it interacts with learned expectations, bias, social identity and incentives."
A number the article refuses to repeat

A neuron is a cell, a synapse a connection, and "A spike, or firing event, is a momentary electrical signal and must be counted over a specified period". Against an estimate of "approximately 86 billion neurons in the adult human brain", the claim of "350 trillion firing neurons" is incorrect. "I will not use neurons, synapses and firing events interchangeably."

Mechanism

Repetition builds the baseline; a salient episode alters the weight; only their coincidence is unusually influential.

A sparkling moment is "an emotionally salient, novel or identity-relevant experience that becomes unusually available as a reference point for later interpretation and action". "“Sparkling” describes intensity and persistence, not goodness."

"Repeated worlds create the baseline: the ordinary pattern of who responds, who disappears, what is praised, what is punished and what a child learns to expect." Sparkling moments "alter the weight".

"Repetition without salience can make a practice familiar. Salience without repetition can make an event memorable. When both coincide, an experience may become especially influential. Even then, influence is a probability, not a verdict."

The author corrects his own earlier use of the idea. In the Durna article "I described a sparkling moment too literally as an intense experience firing a particular neural connection. A human experience is not stored on one isolated wire."

And corrects his own chronology. "My first clearly remembered sparkling moment was not my 1987 encounter with Prof. Sri Margono. It came in 1981", in the air-conditioned library of an American Baptist church, reading magazines about studying in America. "This recollection has not appeared in my published corpus; I record it here for the first time."

Why it is not a sixth percentage

"Nor is the sparkling moment a sixth percentage to be added to 45–22–18–10–5. It is a proposed mechanism for explaining why exposures within those domains are unevenly weighted."

What policy can and cannot do with it

"No ministry can schedule a child’s spark. It can, however, widen the field in which constructive sparks are possible—and reduce the frequency with which fear, neglect and humiliation become the moments a child remembers with greatest force."

The Archive

The archive earns its authority at the points where it refuses to explain.

Across encounters with academics, generals, entrepreneurs and parents, the discipline is in the disclaimers rather than the resemblances.

FIELD NOTE A
Marbles did not produce ethics
On Prof. Djumilah Hadiwidjojo: "The honest inference is not that marbles produced ethical leadership." Play, sibling life and later models "supplied repeated material from which cooperation, persistence and independence could develop".
FIELD NOTE B
A transmitted value is not its method
On General Johnny Lumintang: the line from discipline and shortage to service "does not require us to approve of violence used against a child. Rapid learning under a whip is not evidence that whipping is good pedagogy."
FIELD NOTE C
Adversity is not a training programme
On Francis Ngannou: "Divorce does not manufacture champions. Hardship is not a neurological training programme." The responsible conclusion is "difficulty and care interact".
FIELD NOTE D
The limit case that stays a limit
On Todung Mulya Lubis: "My present archive does not contain enough verified childhood material to tell readers where those convictions began. To invent an origin would weaken both his story and my theory."
The refusal is itself a result

"This boundary-setting is not a failure of the archive. It is one of its findings. Not every admirable adult gives us childhood evidence, and not every childhood story explains the adult."

Framework

Separating five processes stops the child from carrying burdens that belong to the school, market or state.

"The archive became more useful when I separated formation, refinement, consolidation, redirection and testing." "A single life can contain all five."

01
Earliest years
Formation
"the recurring environments and relationships of the earliest years"
02
Childhood to adolescence
Refinement
"the pruning and reorganization that continue through childhood and adolescence"
03
Repeated recruitment
Consolidation
"increasingly efficient use of repeatedly recruited pathways, a process to which continued myelination can contribute"
04
A mentor or institution
Redirection
"a mentor, institution or encounter that makes another route imaginable"
05
Adult pressure
Testing
"the pressure under which an adult either maintains, modifies or abandons a value"

Implications

A country cannot preach character to children and then blame character when adults conform to its institutions.

"If early pathways hold the largest theoretical weight, the practical conclusion is not that parents must engineer future CEOs, generals or professors." That "would replace neglect with another form of violence: treating a child as a project designed to satisfy adult ambition".

The aim is "to increase the probability that widely useful human capacities become practiced before high-stakes life begins": secure attachment, language-rich interaction, curiosity, perspective-taking, emotional regulation, bodily play, "repair after conflict, contact across difference, and experience of rules applied with reasonable consistency".

Moral education changes shape too. "Children need more than instructions to be honest. They need repeated experience of adults telling the truth when truth is inconvenient; rules that do not disappear for powerful families; mistakes followed by repair rather than humiliation."

"Silence does not create neutrality. It leaves children to infer the social order from what adults repeatedly do."

Each remaining weight carries its own instruction: bias means teaching people "to notice the shortcuts of their own minds"; external pressure means "institutions must not demand heroism as their only control system"; character "means personal responsibility remains real but cannot carry the whole explanatory load".

The sentence that does the work

"Put differently: a country cannot preach character to children, expose them to contradictory institutions for twenty years, and then blame individual character when adults conform to those institutions."

And the guard against misuse

"If explanation is mistaken for excuse, prevention becomes impossible. If responsibility is mistaken for a complete causal theory, punishment becomes our only policy."

© 2026 Dr. Toronata Tambun
Evidence

Thirty-six percent enrolment at ages three to six is not a failed system but an unfinanced one.

"Indonesia already possesses important elements of an early-childhood system." PAUD-HI integrates education, health, nutrition, care and protection. The financing does not match.

ON THE RECORD A
Coverage
The 2023 net enrolment rate for children aged three to six was "about 36 percent and about 33 percent among poorer households", with "still lower rates in some eastern provinces".
ON THE RECORD B
Who delivers it
"extensive reliance on privately operated centres, very low pay for many non-civil-service early-childhood educators, limited professional development".
ON THE RECORD C
What cannot be seen
"difficulty tracking spending consistently across ministries and levels of government".
ON THE RECORD D
How to read the figures
"These figures are not evidence that PAUD has failed. They are evidence that the country has not yet financed the formative years in proportion to the importance it assigns them rhetorically."
Why policy cannot start at the school gate

"A child’s first three years are largely spent in households, neighbourhoods, clinics, informal childcare and public space. Rosyid’s first learning environment included a food cart." Policy "must therefore follow the child across settings rather than require the child to appear inside one ministry’s institution before becoming visible".

Recommendations

A Pregnancy-to-Seven Formation Compact would move the policy clock to where the weight actually sits.

Six commitments are proposed. The first four reset the unit of policy; the last two decide whether the investment survives contact with the rest of the system.

1
Make the unit of policy the child-caregiver relationship
Responsive back-and-forth interaction supports early language and brain architecture, but caregivers can provide it "only when they themselves have time, health, safety and support". Without that, "Parenting advice" becomes "moral blame directed at mothers".
2
Connect prenatal care, nutrition, health, protection and early learning
"Pregnancy to age three cannot remain a preface to “real education.”" A child at risk "should not have to be rediscovered separately by every service".
3
Treat PAUD educators as formation professionals
"Raising pay without improving practice is incomplete; demanding quality without raising pay is exploitation."
4
Protect play, and measure environments rather than only enrolment
"Early learning is not a smaller version of secondary school." A dashboard should track "continuity of caregivers and teachers, responsive interaction, safety, inclusion, access to play". "The object is not to rank toddlers."

Early investment is wasted if later schools humiliate curiosity, if universities reward recitation, if workplaces normalize gradual misconduct, or if government demonstrates that rules depend on rank.

— The sixth commitment: childhood is foundational, not sufficient
© 2026 Dr. Toronata Tambun
Conclusion

What Indonesia lacks is not programmes or knowledge but proportionality.

"The biographies in my archive do not prove that the years before seven manufacture destiny. They show why it is irrational to act as though adult character appears from nowhere."

Indonesia does not lack programmes, committed teachers, caring families or knowledge. It lacks proportionality. We continue to invest public urgency after the most developmentally sensitive years, then ask individuals to compensate through motivation, religion, training, discipline or punishment.

— Dr. Toronata Tambun, The Child Before the Leader
He is already a citizen
"But the child is not merely a preliminary version of someone the state will value later." "He is already a citizen whose environment is teaching him what citizenship means."
