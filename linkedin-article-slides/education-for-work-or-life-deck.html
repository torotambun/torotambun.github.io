Education for Work, or Education for Life? Classical pedagogy, Indonesian educational thought, and the cultural conditions of independent judgment

Employability is a necessary but incomplete test of education, because it cannot show whether graduates can judge when familiar instructions fail. Classical liberal arts, cognitive evidence and Indonesia’s own educational precedents all offer resources, yet respected methods do not carry their outcomes with them: whether students learn independent judgment depends on whether institutions reward it or only its appearance.

Dr. Toronata Tambun
Director, Yayasan Mens et Manus
© 2026 Dr. Toronata Tambun
Introduction

A vocational school can prepare students well for jobs and still leave untested whether they can judge when familiar instructions fail.

Continuing The Intelligence-Grade Learning Manual.

Situation

A vocational school offered AI, electrical engineering, instrumentation and industrial chemistry, and employment was a major student aspiration. "This was understandable."

Complication

Asked whether an AFS exchange year "would provide scientific knowledge", the question exposed how educational value was being assessed. Exchange can create "opportunities for questioning assumptions acquired within one's own community."

Question

"What happens when education successfully prepares students for an occupation, but the expected occupation becomes unavailable?"

Answer

"Preparing students for employment remains necessary, but employment alone cannot define the purposes against which education should be evaluated."

Framework

Employability measures qualification, while socialisation and the emergence of an independent agent go largely unmeasured.

Biesta (2009). "Employability names a relation between a person's capabilities and a labour market at a particular time; it does not independently explain what makes an education good."

01
Qualification
Capability to do something
The knowledge and capabilities for a task. Visible in qualifications awarded and jobs obtained.
02
Socialisation
Entry into practices
Entry into existing social and cultural practices, including procedural reliability, safety and established standards.
03
Subjectification
An agent who responds
The person able to respond to those practices "rather than merely reproducing them." Weakly evidenced by conventional indicators.
The narrower point from Aquinas

"the capacity to accomplish a task does not determine whether accomplishing it is justified."

Scope of the observation

"My visit was too limited to establish the school's overall pedagogical character or the intellectual capabilities of its students."

Framework

The trivium and Sayers separate acquiring, criticising and expressing knowledge, so a fluent presentation cannot stand in for understanding.

Grammar, logic and rhetoric, with arithmetic, geometry, astronomy and music: "distinct and connected intellectual demands."

Sayers (1948) mapped the trivium onto Poll-Parrot, Pert and Poetic stages. "In doctoral classrooms, a candidate may reproduce a theory's terminology and present it fluently while remaining unable to reconstruct the theory's premises or identify counterevidence."

"Logic does not acquire educational force merely because the curriculum contains a topic called critical thinking." It needs occasions to question a teacher "without being penalised for the act of questioning." "Rhetoric does not mean confidence of delivery; it requires responsibility for the claims being communicated."

What Sayers is not

"This is Sayers' educational proposal, not a validated developmental law". Classical arrangements "cannot be imported unchanged into Indonesian secondary schools."

Details

Perrin’s eight classical principles rest on unequal evidence, so each must be matched to the capability it is meant to develop.

Perrin (2013) is "a modern synthesis rather than a complete ancient educational canon." "The selection of a teaching method must therefore follow an account of the capability being developed".

Pace, depth and memory
•Festina lente: advance on demonstrated understanding, not syllabus speed
•Multum non multa: depth of examination, without abandoning needed breadth
•Repetitio mater memoriae: retrieval and spacing aid retention, yet "Rehearsal can reinforce an error"
•Songs and jingles: aid recall, not comprehension, and differ from quadrivium music
Engagement, disposition and reflection
•Embodied learning: physical interaction can inform cognition; client visits are better called experiential
•Wonder: starts an investigation without validating its conclusion
•Intellectual humility: applies to educators examining their own methods too
•Scholé: time to contemplate an experience, not merely complete it
Unequal evidence

Retrieval and distributed practice "have substantial empirical support for particular memory outcomes, whereas broader concerns such as intellectual virtue, wisdom and contemplation require philosophical examination alongside empirical investigation."

Evidence

Indonesian educators from Sjafei to Radjiman had already joined practical work, intellectual formation and social responsibility.

As discussed by Alisjahbana (1966, pp. 129–132). "Their work challenges the assumption that Indonesia must choose between imported liberal education and narrowly vocational instruction."

PRECEDENT A
Mohammad Sjafei, INS Kayutanam, 1926
Agriculture and trades alongside music, painting, sculpture and theatre. "Its significance lay in refusing to make manual competence the entire purpose of education."
PRECEDENT B
Ki Hadjar Dewantara, Taman Siswa, 1922
Example, initiative and guidance as capability matures. Authority "must serve an educational purpose beyond obtaining obedience."
PRECEDENT C
Dr. Soetomo
Cooperatives and village financial institutions, broadening education "beyond supplying employees to organisations established by others."
PRECEDENT D
Dr. Radjiman Wedyodiningrat
Individual development with responsibility to society, "without equating independence with disregard for others."
Attributed, not verified

Interpretations are attributed to Alisjahbana, checked against a physical copy. "These historical accounts do not establish that the institutions consistently achieved their intended outcomes".

Details

Sixteen semesters of Babson- and MIT-informed teaching did not consistently form entrepreneurs, showing that respected methods do not carry their outcomes with them.

"Although students encountered situations beyond textbook cases, the entrepreneurial formation I sought did not consistently materialise."

Client visits, guest speakers and pitches each have a limit. "Professional success does not establish the validity of every explanation offered by a practitioner." A pitch "can demonstrate rhetorical fluency while concealing inadequate knowledge of customers, resources or market conditions."

"A teaching method developed in one institutional environment cannot be assumed to produce equivalent outcomes when transferred to another." The same client visit "may encourage independent investigation in one setting while reinforcing dependence on professional authority in another."

Status of the reflection

"These observations constitute firsthand reflective material rather than a controlled comparison of teaching methods, and they cannot establish the independent causal contribution of any individual activity."

© 2026 Dr. Toronata Tambun
Gap

Institutions may display commitment to independent thinking while rewarding agreement, and PISA 2022 shows declared and assessed creativity diverging.

Performative culture "names an emerging construct, not an established, empirically validated national theory."

Koentjaraningrat (1971, p. 38) contrasts attention to the visible front of houses with neglected kitchens and bathrooms; the relevance is analogical. In Schein’s (2010) terms, uniforms and awards are artefacts and critical thinking an espoused value. "If dissent is treated as disrespect and agreement is rewarded irrespective of reasoning, the operative culture may undermine the value publicly declared."

PISA 2022 creative thinking: Indonesia averaged 19 points against an OECD 33, with 31% at baseline proficiency against 78%. Yet 86% said teachers allowed time for creative solutions and 90% that teachers valued creativity (OECD, 2024).

What the evidence cannot show

"The scores do not establish that cultural conformity caused the gap." The two school studies "cannot establish how prevalent the reported practices are across Indonesian education".

Implications

An Indonesian response must assess judgment directly, examine how authority treats evidence, and test locally which methods actually work.

"The choice is not between copying foreign pedagogy and treating earlier Indonesian practices as infallible."

1  ·  Assessment
Students identify weaknesses in unfamiliar arguments and understand when established procedures "may no longer be appropriate."
2  ·  Authority
Teachers and lecturers "distinguish reasoned disagreement from insubordination". "Does the institution investigate the claim, or does it demand conformity?"
3  ·  Design
Liberal arts, cognitive evidence and Indonesian precedents tested empirically "through learner outcomes and observable institutional behaviour."
4  ·  Four outcomes
Work competently, think independently with adequate knowledge, judge responsibly, adapt through further learning. "These are not a validated sequence".
© 2026 Dr. Toronata Tambun
Conclusion

Employment remains a real achievement, but a school should be able to show what its graduates can do when a procedure fails.

"employment outcomes cannot be discarded, but neither should they close the evaluation of education. A graduate's first job can represent a substantial material achievement."

At the same time, an educational institution should be able to show what the graduate understands about the work, what can be done when a procedure fails and how new knowledge can be acquired when occupational demands change.

— Dr. Toronata Tambun, Education for Work, or Education for Life?
How the position changed
The vocational visit, the teaching batches, doctoral research and work on memory each moved the argument. The four outcomes "specify outcomes that a school should not claim to have produced without evidence."
