From Consulting Claims to Research Questions: Learning Before Newness

Dissecting a consulting report rather than learning its recommendations teaches doctoral students to move from a claim to a question worth answering: reconstruct the proposed relationships, compare them with theory, test them against full-text research and accept that the answer may already exist. BCG’s booklet survives as qualified and useful, and the exercise shows that learning must precede Recall, Forget and Newness, with newness earned by argument rather than asserted.

Dr. Toronata Tambun
Director, Yayasan Mens et Manus

Introduction

A slide deck that summarised BCG’s risk booklet missed the point, which was to examine the reasoning behind its recommendations.

"Why am I asking doctoral students to dissect a consulting report rather than simply learn its recommendations?"

Situation

Preparing a Governance, Risk, Compliance, and Sustainability class with ChatGPT, slides were requested on BCG’s The Art of Risk Management (Pidun et al., 2017).

Complication

"The first treatment summarized the booklet. I wanted the class to examine the reasoning behind the recommendations and the conditions under which they might work".

Question

"How can they move from understanding an existing argument to developing a question whose answer would make a difference?"

Answer

A disciplined workflow "for comparing an article with theory, examining the resulting questions against research, and deciding what still warrants investigation."

© 2026 Dr. Toronata Tambun
Framework

The workflow separates observation from causal claim, reconstructs the mechanism, then tests the resulting question against full-text research.

"This is my teaching arrangement of established scholarly practices, not a claim to have invented a new research method." It is consistent with problematization (Alvesson & Sandberg, 2011).

1  ·  Separate and reconstruct
Distinguish an observation from a recommendation or causal explanation; ask "what action is expected to affect which outcome, through what mechanism, and under what conditions?"
2  ·  First reflection
Challenge the article’s assumptions or inference against relevant theory, which tests explanatory fit, not empirical validity.
3  ·  Search and read in full
Use research libraries and tools such as scite. "Abstracts help select papers. They cannot replace examination of the complete argument and evidence."
4  ·  Second reflection and decision
Answered within scope, partially answered, or inconclusive. Every candidate faces "the “so what?” test" and another search.
© 2026 Dr. Toronata Tambun
Evidence

In the BCG case, a challenge to leadership support was sharpened by existing research into a narrower question about who receives the warning.

BCG’s April 2017 booklet offers ten principles and historical company examples. The analysis "does not infer the authors’ competence, motives, or research process from the number of academic citations appearing in the booklet."

Table 1 moved from recommendation to theoretical challenge: "Leadership support can be useful, but asking who constrains the leader exposes a possible condition on that usefulness." Table 2 then examined what research already said. "Research on reporting arrangements already exists."

The possible next step concerns "whether the recipient of an escalated warning has sufficient independence from the same controller." If the literature answers even that, "the student should learn from the answer and revise the proposed contribution."

Status of the worked example

"This worked example remains an exploratory review." The Souza, Feldman and Denrell entries remain abstract-based, and Table 2 "does not represent completion of the full-paper review required by the proposed workflow."

© 2026 Dr. Toronata Tambun
Details

The assessment of BCG is qualified and positive, because an unresolved challenge does not make advice wrong and a related paper does not make it right.

"The booklet provides useful guidance with credible theoretical connections and some relevant empirical support."

What the review supports
✓Useful guidance connecting risk management with operating decisions and judgment
✓Credible theoretical connections and some empirical support
✓"An unresolved challenge does not make a recommendation wrong. It may identify a condition needed for the recommendation to work."
What it does not establish
✗"that all ten recommendations have been validated as a complete intervention"
✗Universal endorsement: Landier et al. (2009) show incentives can undermine independent oversight, which "helps specify a boundary"
✗What BCG’s authors knew in 2017; later studies inform only the current assessment

Implications

When an engagement costs millions, the client should be able to see how evidence, judgment and its own circumstances connect.

"The fee itself cannot establish the scientific strength of the recommendation."

"BCG’s practitioner synthesis and a doctoral literature review serve different purposes. The report need not reproduce the literature review on every page." The class reconstructs that background so students can assess its application.

The questions raised "may improve an application of the advice, while a fuller literature review may resolve some of the questions already raised." "The task is to reach a defensible conclusion, not to preserve a preferred novelty claim."

© 2026 Dr. Toronata Tambun
Framework

Learning must come before Recall, Forget and Newness, and a shorter citation list alone does not show that forgetting or newness occurred.

Prof. PM Laksono’s anthropological dialectic, as described in an earlier series (Tambun, 2026a, 2026b, 2026c).

Learning
Competence through practice
"Reading and applying theory provide knowledge that can later be recalled, questioned, and revised".
Recall
Earlier understanding made examinable
Theory helps explain a recommendation.
Forget
Authority loosened
Comparison with evidence may loosen an assumption about the recommendation’s scope.
Newness
Revised understanding
A better explanation, a more precise recommendation or a research question. "New understanding for a learner or a client is not automatically a novel contribution to scholarship".
On BCG and the dialectic

"The booklet alone cannot establish the authors’ internal learning process, so I cannot assign BCG to a particular stage of the dialectic." The Sterman advice is given as recollection, "not a verbatim quotation."

© 2026 Dr. Toronata Tambun
Conclusion

Newness has to earn its place through an argument that stays open to further learning.

"This means that the tables are a record of developing judgment, not a certificate that thinking has finished."

Learning supplies the material for Recall, while Forget permits its authority to be reconsidered. Newness must then earn its place through an argument that remains open to further learning.

— Dr. Toronata Tambun, From Consulting Claims to Research Questions
The closing questions
"What has been learned well enough to recall? Which assumption can now be loosened for a defensible reason? What new understanding follows, and what decision or explanation would it change?"
Analytical levels, not rankings
TARIF materials separate broad foundations, governance explanations and microscopic mechanisms. "They should not be treated as rigid rankings, or used to label every narrow construct a low-range theory".
