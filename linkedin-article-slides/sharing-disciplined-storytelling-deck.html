Sharing Disciplined Storytelling with Those Who Inspired My Journey

Storytelling is neither a talent nor an accessory to teaching. It is a discipline whose foundation is laid in the first twelve years of education, sharpened by writing, and worth nothing at all unless the idea inside the story has depth.

Dr. Toronata Tambun
Director, Yayasan Mens et Manus
© 2026 Dr. Toronata Tambun
Introduction

A lecture full of data lost the room until I told the story of my own failed venture.

A photograph taken in Boston in August 2024 is the occasion for the reflection; a classroom years earlier is where the argument starts.

Situation

I had spent hours preparing a lecture on the role of ecosystems in entrepreneurship, filled with data and analysis, for students I expected to reach.

Complication

As I began speaking the students were disengaged. Something was missing, and the material was not what was missing.

Question

What changed when a personal story about a failed venture of my own entered the room?

Answer

The data acquired meaning because it was attached to a narrative. Storytelling was not an accessory to the teaching; it was a central component of it.

© 2026 Dr. Toronata Tambun
Background

The habit behind that story was formed in borrowed libraries, not in a classroom.

The journey into storytelling started far earlier than I realised.

Growing up in a competitive academic environment in Jakarta, the value of a disciplined mind was instilled early. My father's position was a refusal and a provision at the same time: "I don't provide you money, but I provide you knowledge through books" — words that echo still.

The libraries were borrowed ones: elementary, junior high and Smandel's school library, the LIPI Round Building's library, Kebayoran Baptist church's library, and a rich friend's library. The books were borrowed too, and returned.

The reading happened in the gaps rather than at a desk — on the KRL train, in a metro-mini, on a minibus, while attending my mother's shop, while waiting for a piano lesson, in the contemplation after school and after Maghrib. That foundation of curiosity and disciplined thinking still shapes how I teach, write and lead.

Why this belongs in the argument

The discipline being described is not a technique acquired for the classroom. It is a reading habit formed under constraint, and the article treats it as the reason the storytelling later held together.

Framework

Storytelling carries knowledge because knowledge is co-constructed, not because a story entertains.

Three positions, each doing a different part of the work.

01
Vygotsky, 1978
Social constructivism
Knowledge is co-constructed through social interaction. Storytelling is the medium of that transfer, bridging cognitive development and social understanding rather than decorating it.
02
Winston, 2021
Story as scaffolding
The ability to tell a compelling story is among the most powerful ways to communicate an idea. A story is not only a narrative tool but a way of scaffolding thought in the mind of another person.
03
Bandura, 1977
Self-efficacy
Belief in one's own ability to succeed shapes how a task is approached. As students build confidence in storytelling through disciplined practice, performance follows the belief.
© 2026 Dr. Toronata Tambun
Details

Writing sharpens a story and mind mapping makes it buildable; neither is optional.

The disciplines that sit underneath the delivery.

1  ·  Writing is a method, not a technical skill
Winston argues in Make It Clear that writing improves the clarity of ideas rather than merely recording them. Writing, revising and refining force the thought to sharpen. Great storytellers are also great writers.
2  ·  Mind mapping gives the story a structure before delivery
Buzan's technique helps students break complex ideas into structured visual formats and build coherent narratives, which is what thoughtful preparation and idea clarity require.
3  ·  Slow thinking is what keeps storytelling deliberate
Kahneman's distinction between fast and slow thinking places storytelling on the slow side: deliberate reflection and careful crafting, in place of impulsive telling.
© 2026 Dr. Toronata Tambun
Evidence

Structured communication deepened analysis most for the introverts it was assumed to disadvantage.

Three lines of support, one of them a study of the framework itself.

EVIDENCE A
The study of the framework
Tambun, Yudoko and Aldianto (2024) found that structured communication, especially among introverts, enhanced both the depth of analysis and the ability to articulate complex ideas.
EVIDENCE B
The classroom shift
Students who once struggled with the concept of market validation grasp it more fully when it is framed through real-world stories of resilience, failure and learning — moving from conscious incompetence to conscious competence.
EVIDENCE C
The published argument
The Jakarta Post piece on tools for introverts holds that Indonesian education tends to favour extroverted communication styles, and that introverted students can excel through storytelling and other structured techniques.
© 2026 Dr. Toronata Tambun
Implications

Indonesia's oral tradition supplies the storytelling and withholds the discipline.

The cultural setting is favourable and incomplete at the same time.

In Indonesian culture, where oral traditions have long held sway, storytelling is deeply rooted (Geertz, 1961). What is often overlooked is the discipline behind it — which is precisely the part that has to be taught.

Collaboration with educators such as George Whitfield and Leo Aldianto has shown how storytelling can be woven into the fabric of a university's entrepreneurship programme rather than left to individual flair. In the corporate-backed, university-based entrepreneurship models being developed, storytelling, writing and the quality of ideas become the vehicles through which students reach investors and customers alike.

Conclusion

A story carrying a weak idea is still a weak idea.

Where the argument lands, and why it lands earlier than higher education.

the ability to tell a story or write well is meaningless if the underlying idea lacks depth, creativity, or value

— Sharing Disciplined Storytelling with Those Who Inspired My Journey, 22 September 2024
The quality of the idea is the binding constraint
Storytelling and writing are crucial, but what determines impact is the quality of the idea being communicated. The order matters: structure and clarity carry an idea; they do not manufacture one.
Which puts the decisive years before university
The author's own book argues that the first twelve years of education shape the quality of ideas a student later develops — the period in which the mental models relied on in higher education are formed.
And makes the skill a practice, not an event
Storytelling practised with discipline is not an overnight skill. It is built through years of mindful reflection, practice and knowledge development, and refined in collaboration with others.
