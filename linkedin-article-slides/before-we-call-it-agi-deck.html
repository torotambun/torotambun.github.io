Before We Call It AGI: A Clearer Map of Artificial Intelligence

ANI, AGI and ASI, the methods onion, model classes and agents answer different questions. Classifying an AI system on four axes, and asking which axis a label names, makes claims about it evaluable without requiring mastery of every architecture.

Dr. Toronata Tambun
Director, Yayasan Mens et Manus
© 2026 Dr. Toronata Tambun
Introduction

AI labels describe different things, and treating them as interchangeable weakens the evaluation of AI claims.

"It is easy to begin using an AI tool. Understanding what its capabilities mean takes more care."

Situation

"We encounter labels such as machine learning, deep learning, generative AI, large language models and agents." Then come three more: ANI, AGI and ASI.

Complication

"Treating them as interchangeable makes it harder to evaluate both the technology and the claims made about it."

Question

What does each label actually describe?

Answer

"These labels describe different things." "Some concern the breadth of a system’s capabilities. Others describe how it is built, what it does or what kinds of information it processes."

© 2026 Dr. Toronata Tambun
Framework

Narrow, general and super intelligence describe breadth of capability, and generative AI alone does not establish general intelligence.

"These are useful working concepts, not a timetable or a guaranteed sequence of development."

01
ANI
Artificial narrow intelligence
Intelligence specialised in particular tasks or domains, such as a spam filter. "It does not necessarily mean weak performance; a specialised system may outperform people at its particular task."
02
AGI
Artificial general intelligence
Broad, adaptable competence across many cognitive tasks. "However, the definition and the threshold for establishing AGI remain debated."
03
ASI
Artificial superintelligence
Hypothetical intelligence substantially beyond human cognitive capability across most domains. "Exceptional performance on one specialised task would not, by itself, establish ASI."
The distinction that deserves particular attention

Generative AI does not, by itself, establish AGI. "A fluent response demonstrates a form of performance; it does not settle the larger question of general capability".

© 2026 Dr. Toronata Tambun
Gap

The methods onion shows how approaches nest, but language tasks and generation cross its layers instead of adding rings.

"The onion remains useful for the subset relationship among AI, machine learning, neural networks and deep learning. It does not answer every classification question."

What the onion shows
✓AI is the broad field, machine learning is one approach within it, and neural networks are a family of models used in machine learning
✓Deep learning uses multiple processing layers to learn representations
✓Transformers are one architecture, and "they are not a level of general intelligence"
✓Symbolic AI and hybrid neuro-symbolic systems sit alongside learned methods
What the onion cannot show
✗NLP "therefore crosses the layers rather than occupying one small inner circle"
✗"Language tasks, content generation, decision making and agent action should not be drawn as further exclusive rings."
✗"Generating content and making predictions are not mutually exclusive."
✗"Prediction is part of the mechanism through which generation occurs"
© 2026 Dr. Toronata Tambun
The Four Questions

Four questions classify an AI system better than additional rings, and the answers can coexist in one system.

"How broad is its capability? How is it built? What sort of model or system is it? What function does it perform?"

1  ·  Capability
ANI, AGI or ASI. Breadth of capability, and nothing about how the system is built.
2  ·  Construction
Symbolic rules or machine learning. Within machine learning, neural networks may be shallow or deep, and transformers are one deep architecture.
3  ·  Model or system class
Classifiers, decision models, foundation models, generative models, LLMs, multimodal systems, reasoning models and agents.
4  ·  Function
Prediction, generation, reasoning, decision support and action.
Status of the framework

"the four questions are a teaching framework rather than a universal standard."

© 2026 Dr. Toronata Tambun
Evidence

Jev, a vendor-documented decision model, stress-tests the framework, and its accuracy and architecture remain unverified.

The vendor, TypeSafe AI, calls it a System One Model. The deck reports what the documentation says and what the article says it does not establish.

JEV A
What the documentation says
Jev takes a supplied state and bounded questions, then returns typed choices, rubric scores or probabilities. The company says it does not generate open-ended text.
JEV B
What that documentation cannot show
"These sources document the advertised interface. They do not independently verify accuracy, calibration, speed or internal architecture".
JEV C
Where it sits in the taxonomy
"A decision model is a functional model class, alongside classifiers, scoring models and learned policies. It is not a new layer above deep learning."
JEV D
Where the decision actually sits
"A model may choose among labels while the consequential decision remains with code or a person." And "A probability field is no proof of calibration."
On the vendor's product term

"“System One Model” remains TypeSafe AI’s product term. Its analogy to psychological System 1 and System 2 is not evidence of a settled technical taxonomy."

Details

A model is not the system people use, and an agentic label does not establish general intelligence.

The distinction between a model and the assembled system matters when a result is attributed to intelligence.

"An LLM can be one component of an assistant. The complete system may include an interface, retrieval, other models and tools." Adding memory and planning can support agent workflows, but "the label alone does not establish general intelligence".

"A foundation model is trained on broad data and can be adapted to a range of tasks", and those tasks include prediction as well as generation. Multimodal describes models or systems that combine kinds of data such as text, images and audio. "Neither term is a synonym for AGI".

© 2026 Dr. Toronata Tambun
Conclusion

A claim about AI should name the axis its label describes and the evidence that supports it on unfamiliar tasks.

"The practical consequence is simple. When someone makes a claim about AI, ask which axis the label names."

Mastering every architecture is unnecessary before trying an AI tool. Enough conceptual clarity is necessary to describe its results responsibly.

— Dr. Toronata Tambun, Before We Call It AGI
For any AI claim
"A claim should specify the tasks demonstrated, their reliability in unfamiliar situations, and the role of tools, retrieved information or human intervention."
For decision services
Ask "what is scored, who sets the objective and threshold, who authorises action, and whether probabilities have been checked on relevant cases."
On the illustrations
"These are original teaching syntheses, not reproduced journal figures." The five figures in the article are not reproduced in this deck.
