The Republic of Indonesia v. Nadiem Anwar Makarim (Case No. 147/PITSUS-TPK/2025/PN JKT.PST): A Pseudo Legal Analysis Report

Two cases are running inside one trial. On the procurement, the August 2020 Windows message is genuine exculpatory evidence against mens rea. On the wealth, Article 37 reverses the burden and intent is not required. The report's prediction therefore turns on the second, not the first — and it is a prediction, not a verdict.

Director, Yayasan Mens et Manus

Introduction

Two cases are being tried at once, and they point in opposite directions.

An analysis of the prosecution's rebuttal (Replik) and the defendant's response (Duplik), as presented at the Jakarta Central District Court.

Situation

The prosecution has constructed a narrative of "Special Systematic" corruption, arguing the defendant used ministerial authority to validate a pre-existing evil consensus (mufakat jahat) with Google. The defence answers with a mission of software-driven transformation, a fiscal saving, and a WhatsApp message it calls exculpatory.

Complication

The two sides are not arguing about the same thing. One case concerns the Chromebook procurement and whether the minister intended to enrich a commercial partner. The other concerns a wealth increase of IDR 4,871,469,603,758 during his term and whether he can account for it.

Question

If the procurement case and the wealth case are answered by different evidence and different doctrines, which one carries the trial?

Answer

The wealth case. Intent is contested on the procurement and the defence has real evidence there. On the wealth, Articles 37 and 37A shift the burden onto the defendant, and the report finds that burden undischarged.

The Prosecution

The state alleges one scheme running from retained shares to a IDR 4.87 trillion wealth increase.

The Replik distils the state's case into eight definitive legal violations. They are presented here as allegations argued at trial.

1  ·  Systemic conflict of interest
"Intentional retention of PT AKAP (GoTo) shares to profit from Google-centric educational policies." The prosecution relies on Identification Theory, as articulated by Prof. Nindyo Pramono, to argue the defendant remained the directing mind despite the irrevocable power of attorney.
2  ·  Shadow governance and administrative betrayal
"Implementation of a "Shadow Organization" (Tan, Arif, Handayani) that overrode official structural roles of Echelon I and II officers", together with the "Intentional replacement of competent officials (Hamim/Popi) with nominees willing to sign off on the Chrome OS pivot."
3  ·  Procedural fraud and coercive policy-making
"Deliberate manipulation of notarial records and tax filings (SPT) to mask the true scale of the defendant's financial relationship with Google", and the bypassing of school needs identification to lock technical specifications through Permendikbud No. 5/2021, "ensuring a Chrome OS monopoly."
4  ·  Economic enrichment
"Orchestrating a IDR 809.6 Billion "corporate action" within Gojek Indonesia that functioned as a disguised kickback."
5  ·  Constitutional failure
"Deepening the educational gap in 3T regions by deploying internet-dependent hardware, a direct "betrayal of the constitutional mandate" for equal education."
6  ·  Unexplained wealth
"Failure to utilize the Article 37 (Reverse Burden of Proof) opportunity to explain a IDR 4.87 Trillion wealth increase during tenure." Witnesses Andre Soelistyo and Kevin Aluwi testified under oath that they were required to report to and seek approval from the defendant for critical corporate actions.

The Defence

The defence answers with a software mission, a IDR 3.9 trillion saving, and one WhatsApp message.

The Duplik contrasts the prosecution's faceless legalism with the personal stakes, and then makes three substantive arguments about the procurement.

What the Prosecution Says the Policy Was
•A hardware procurement locked to Chrome OS
•Specifications coerced past technical warnings
•A monopoly secured for a commercial partner
•Devices idled ten months a year at a cost of IDR 21 billion in CDM fees
What the Defence Says It Was
•A software transformation — PMM, Arkas, Siplah — with hardware a minor enabler
•Chromebooks only 1 of 11 initiatives in Google's February 2020 presentation
•Chrome OS selected as fiduciary duty to the budget during the COVID-19 fiscal crisis
•A leap without which transforming 50 million students would have taken 2 to 3 decades
The August 11, 2020 "Windows Pivot"

A WhatsApp chat between the defendant and Ibrahim Arif, offered to prove a lack of mens rea. The defendant instructed: "If we allow schools to buy PC [Windows] notebooks, it's not the end of the world, right? I will choose the former [everyone having laptops] any day." The defence argues this shows he was prepared to abandon Chrome OS exclusivity to ensure students received devices during the supply chain crisis.

The Human Argument

"I am a human being. I am a son who worries about the health of both my parents who are now over 80 years old. I am a father who worries about the fate of my four children if they are separated from me again... I am not just a sheet of paper; I am a human with feelings and a family holding their breath every day waiting for justice."

The Financial Record

IDR 11 trillion arrived from Google; IDR 72 billion was recorded in the deeds.

Invoking the doctrine of Substance Over Form (Dr. Mei Jati), the prosecution places actual cash inflows against notarial recordation. These are the figures the court is asked to weigh.

ON RECORD A
The recordation gap
Actual cash inflows from Google of approximately IDR 11 trillion — Google Asia Pacific investments into the PT AKAP/GoTo ecosystem — against "Only IDR 72 Billion recorded across multiple deeds (Notary Jose Dima Satria)." "This IDR 10.9 Trillion "gap" represents the true price of the policy influence sold to Google."
ON RECORD B
The SPT classification
"The IDR 5.2 Trillion share valuation in the 2022 SPT was recorded in the "Stock Sales" column (penjualan saham) rather than the "End of Year Assets" column." The prosecution argues this was a deliberate attempt to legitimize cash inflows under the guise of stock movements that never actually occurred on the exchange.
ON RECORD C
Offshore holdings
Three Bank of Singapore accounts were presented — IDR 1,279,701,676; IDR 3,631,757,317; IDR 1,822,140,952 — together with Planet Ocean Pte. Ltd., "An offshore vehicle the prosecution alleges was used to bypass Indonesian disclosure requirements for investments and kickbacks."
ON RECORD D
The 0.15 percent utility metric
Dashboard evidence from admin.belajar.id, supported by testimony from Muhammad Hasan Habibi, "showed that out of 1.6 million Chromebooks, only 0.15% were logged into for regular learning activities." Usage peaked exclusively during the October–November AKM window, leading the prosecution to label IDR 21 billion in CDM fees a state loss for devices idled ten months a year.

The Doctrinal Clash

Whether this is administrative error or corruption turns on Article 603 against lex specialis.

A central legal battle in this trial is whether the New Criminal Code mandates that administrative sanctions be prioritized over criminal prosecution.

The defence argues that under the new criminal-law paradigm the principle of Primum Remedium should apply, and that "any procedural lapses in the Chromebook procurement were administrative errors that should be handled through government oversight, not a criminal court."

The prosecution asserts that notwithstanding the administrative provisions in the 2023 Code, the Anti-Corruption Law remains a lex specialis that overrides general administrative rules, that "Article 603 of the new code does not revoke the "extraordinary" status of corruption crimes", and that administrative mercy applies only to pure errors of negligence.

The trial also tests the limits of the presumption of innocence against statutory reversal. "Under Articles 37 and 37A, the legal burden shifts: the defendant is obligated to prove that assets which do not align with their official income (such as his Singaporean bank accounts and the IDR 5.2 trillion recorded in his 2022 SPT) were obtained legally." The defendant maintains the increase is a product of entrepreneurial success and asks the judge to look beyond the profile on paper.

A Note on the Amicus Submissions

"The Court has received five physical submissions for Amicus Curiae. However, judicial analysis reveals they represent only three distinct content groups, with the final three submissions being word-for-word identical." The report reads this as coordinated institutional pressure rather than spontaneous public outcry — a factor bearing on judicial independence, and one the article raises rather than resolves.

The Two Pathways

The Windows chat negates intent on procurement; Article 37 does not require intent on wealth.

The report sets out two pathways to verdict, each with its legal centrepiece, its logic, and its sentence prediction.

Pathway B — Acquittal
•Centrepiece: Article 603 of Law No. 1/2023 and Primum Remedium (Dr. Ahmad Redi)
•The August 11, 2020 Windows Pivot chat as the smoking gun of innocence
•Chrome OS rollout errors as administrative negligence, not administrative error with criminal dimension
•Prediction: full acquittal, or dismissal in favour of administrative sanctions
Pathway A — Guilty
•Centrepiece: Article 37 of Law No. 31/1999, reverse burden of proof
•The unexplained IDR 4.87 trillion growth and the Stock Sales SPT discrepancy
•Directing-mind control over PT AKAP as a proven conflict of interest
•Prediction: 18 years' imprisonment and total forfeiture of the unexplained wealth
Hero Versus Criminal

The court is balancing two competing archetypes: "A young idealist who left a multi-billion dollar private career to disrupt a stagnant bureaucracy and save the state IDR 3.9 Trillion", against "A high-status "predatory" official who used the "Image" strategy to mask a classic layering and fraud scheme."

Conclusion

The prediction rests on the wealth, not on the Chromebooks.

The report's own concluding judgment, offered as a reading of where the legal weight sits under current Indonesian law.

While the "Windows Pivot" chat strongly negates mens rea for the procurement itself, the defendant's inability to explain the multi-trillion rupiah wealth growth and the irregular SPT reporting provides the Court with sufficient "material facts" to find a violation of the Anti-Corruption Law.

— The report's prediction statement
The trigger is procedural, not evidentiary
"Under current Indonesian law, the Prosecution's case holds superior weight due to the legal trigger of Article 37 (Reverse Burden of Proof)." The prosecution's advantage on this reading comes from where the law places the burden, not from the strength of its procurement evidence.
The condition on which it turns
"The "Special Systematic" nature of the wealth discrepancy makes an acquittal unlikely unless the defense can immediately prove the legitimate source of the 4.87T assets." The prediction is conditional and states its own defeater.
What this analysis does not claim
It does not establish that the allegations are true, that the transcripts are complete, or that the court will decide as predicted.
