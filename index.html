Welcome — and thank you for spending a few minutes of a busy day here.

My work sits at the intersection of entrepreneurial formation, institutional culture, judgment under uncertainty, and disciplined human development.

I am Executive Director of Yayasan Mens et Manus (Jakarta) and Founder and Director of Aren Energy Investment Pte Ltd (Singapore). I bring over 24 years of executive experience across 52 countries, including thirteen years with Schlumberger in regional and global roles spanning operations, technology deployment, and organizational leadership — the fuller record is on the Industry Experience page.

I completed the Doctoral Program in Management Science at Institut Teknologi Bandung (2023–2026), graduating with the degree Doktor (Dr.), Doctor of Philosophy — Summa Cum Laude, GPA 4.0 of 4.0, Minimum Required Time for Graduation. My doctoral theory — the Dynamic Feedback Theory of Entrepreneurial Formation in Loss-Averse, Short-Horizon Cultures — explains why innovation ecosystems in Indonesia fail not from resource scarcity, but from suppressed feedback loops, cultural risk-aversion, and institutional conformity.

Earlier formation includes a Bachelor of Engineering in Nuclear Engineering — Sarjana Teknik — from Universitas Gadjah Mada (1994), a dual MM/MBA from IPMI Jakarta and Monash University Melbourne (2001), executive education at Harvard Business School (GMP26, Full Alumni, 2018–2019), the Private Equity School at Saïd Business School, University of Oxford (Associate Alumni, 2017), and fourteen courses at MIT Sloan (ACE, Affiliate Alumni, 2016–2018) — the fuller record of programs and certificates is on the Courses and Executive Education pages.

I am a columnist at The Jakarta Post, where I have written 87 columns — to see the complete list, please visit my Jakarta Post Columns archive. I also keep a running blog on LinkedIn Articles and, as an autoethnographer, publish Field Notes drawn from daily practice — to see the complete list of either, please visit LinkedIn Articles or Field Notes. This sits alongside my academic writing — to see the complete list, please visit the Scholarly Article section below.

I am a member of the Harvard Business Review Advisory Council and an MIT En-Roads Climate Simulation Ambassador. I am available for board advisory, research collaboration, and speaking engagements. I am based across Jakarta and Singapore.
